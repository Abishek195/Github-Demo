Event log

Today: "Send photo?"

  • Same generic text in every chat, even when a photo can't help (refund status, opening hours).
  • Doesn't say what to photograph, so customers send the wrong shot and agents have to ask again.
  • Nothing checks the photo for blur, relevance, or a visible address before it's sent.
  • Dismissing it doesn't teach it anything.

Prototype: Claude-guided nudge

  • Shows only when it helps. Claude gives a yes or no with a confidence score, and stays silent when a photo isn't useful.
  • Asks for a specific photo, e.g. "Photo of the damaged box?" instead of "Send photo?".
  • A capture checklist tells the customer what the agent needs to see.
  • Pre-send check for relevance, quality, and privacy, with a suggested retake.
  • Auto-drafted caption with the context the agent needs, so the case moves faster.
  • Respects ×. Once dismissed, it stays quiet for the rest of that chat.

Concept prototype. Stridewell Sneakers is a made-up store. When Claude isn't available in this view, keyword rules take over and the panel says so. Photos you pick go to Claude only when you tap "Check photo" and are never stored.
